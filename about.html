Meet Nina Thompson & Our Story

Hi there! I’m Nina Thompson, founder and lead creative behind Nina Thompson Salon. I started this salon with a simple vision: to create a space where everyone feels empowered to embrace bold, authentic self-expression.

With over 10 years of experience in color transformations and modern styling, my passion is helping every client walk out feeling confident, radiant, and full of positive vibes!

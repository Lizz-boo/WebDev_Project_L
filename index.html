Beauty & Hair Care Tips

1. Protect Your Hair from Heat

Always apply a heat protectant spray before using flat irons or curling wands to keep your hair healthy and split-end free.

2. Extend Your Color's Life

Wash your hair with sulfate-free cold or lukewarm water to keep vibrant colors from fading quickly.

3. Scalp Care is Hair Care

Massage your scalp for 3 minutes daily to promote blood flow and stimulate healthy hair growth.
